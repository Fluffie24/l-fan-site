    PIXEL FIELD GUIDE

    FLY & COLLECT

    • Pixel Chibi L & Black Cat Flaps with GE6 feather pin; Black Cat sprouts Cat-Bunny ears in Fever!
    • Sky & Purple Dream Rings L's lightstick colors (+100 pts, builds Combo & Fever).
    • Tornado Potato (มันฝรั่งเกลียว) L's favorite snack (+300 pts & +22% Fever boost).
    • Wing of the Heart (心の羽根) Restores 1 lost Wing HP (or +400 bonus at full HP).

    DODGE HAZARDS

    • Thunder Storm Cloud Heavy static cloud blocking a sky lane.
    • Headwind Gale (風に逆らい) Fast gust telegraphed by a pulsing “!” edge marker.
    • Night Bat (ค้างคาว) One of L's feared critters fluttering across the dark sky.
    • Leaping Catfish (ปลาดุก) Splashes up into the lower lane during ocean crossings!

    HOW TO PLAY · WATARIDORI DASH

    Controls

    • Keyboard: Press ↑/↓ or W/S to switch sky lanes, 1/2/3 to jump straight to Top/Mid/Low lane, and P to pause.
    • Touch / Mouse: Tap any lane on the sky canvas, swipe up/down, or use the LANE UP / LANE DOWN arcade buttons below the screen.

    Scoring & Dream Fever

    • Collecting Dream Rings & Tornado Potatoes raises your Combo multiplier (up to x5) and fills the Dream Fever gauge.
    • At 100% Fever, Chibi L becomes invincible for 7 seconds with 2x points, gentle ring magnetism, and Cat-Bunny ears on her Black Cat companion!
    • Surviving all 4 lyric chapters completes a Season loop for a Continent Milestone Bonus.

    Audio & Leaderboard

    BGM plays Wataridoritachi ni Sora wa Mienai (assets/Wataridoritachi.mp3) when you start or toggle BGM. After any flight ends, enter any display name (1–18 characters) to record your score without logging in.
